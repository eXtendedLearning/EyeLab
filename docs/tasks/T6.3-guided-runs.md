# T6.3 — Guided runs with audio cues; motion sync, loop closure, stereo pan (gates B1t, B2, BS-2)

> Proposed 2026-11-18 after the T6.2 session (`T6.2-head-pose-and-timecode.md`, Results); accepted by Jack the
> same day. Built as 0.4.003 and installed on the Beam Pro; not run yet. Analyser: `python -m xreal_gates bp RUN`
> (`xreal_gates/guided.py`, tested on synthetic runs: the offset fit recovers a known exposure offset to 1 ms).
> Run length rule (Jack, 2026-11-18): every run ≤ 3 min, normally 1-2.

## Why

- **Jack:** he wants to watch the glasses, not the clock or the Beam Pro screen. The T6.2 head run had no
  usable still window because the moves were not followed, and the relocalisation jumps cannot be judged
  without knowing whether he stood back on the start spot.
- **B1t:** the timecode bounds exposure → `cam_ts` only together with the Beam Pro display latency. Head
  motion seen by the Eye (image shift) against the head pose (IMU-driven, `GetHeadPoseAtTime`) gives the
  exposure time without any display.
- **BS-2:** the rear pair opens, but its stamps are synthetic; only image motion shows whether the two
  exposures are simultaneous.
- **Glasses view:** shimmering square, late first content.

## Changes (one APK)

| change | detail |
|---|---|
| **Cue scripts** (new) | each run is a list of steps (instruction, seconds). At each step: a spoken prompt (Android `TextToSpeech`, English; if no engine is available, beeps only, logged), a tone, the instruction large in the glasses and on the Beam Pro screen, a bar for the step's time left. Three ticks before a move. A long tone at the end. 5 s "get ready" after the button. Every step start is logged in `events.csv` (`cue,<step>`), so the analyser cuts the run into still / turn / walk phases |
| **Head 6DoF loop** (Eye on, as in use) | cued: still 15 s on a marked spot → look left, right, up, down → turn 90° left, back, 90° right, back → walk 2 m forward, turn, walk back to the mark, face the start direction → still 15 s. Analyser: still jitter per still phase, turn angle read vs 90°, **loop closure** (end − start position) and every jump with its phase. About 100 s |
| **Motion sync** (Eye + head) | cued: face a textured wall or the board 1-2 m away, still 5 s, shake left-right 12 s and nod up-down 8 s, about once a second (a soft tick every half second), still between. Saves every frame at 320x180 and the native head pose every 5 ms of HMD time (`pose_grid.csv`). Analyser: image shift between consecutive frames (phase correlation) fitted linearly against the head rotation between the two frame times shifted by a trial offset; the offset with the best fit (R²) is exposure − `cam_ts`. 38 s |
| **Stereo pan** | Beam Pro in hand, rear cameras at a textured scene; cued: still 4 s, pan left-right slowly 10 s, quickly 10 s. Saves every image of both sensors at 160x90 (`stereo_thumbs_<id>.bin`) and records the Beam Pro gyroscope (`imu.csv`). Analyser: the same fit per sensor against the integrated gyroscope; the difference of the two offsets is the exposure offset between the sensors. 33 s |
| **Glasses view** | Android pipeline asset: MSAA 4x and render scale 1.0 (now 0.8, off), recorded in CONTEXT.md as a Beam Pro setting; the square drawn from a soft-edged texture with mipmaps; a **Place square** button puts it 1.5 m ahead again; "EyeLab starting" shown from the first rendered frame |
| **App log file** | every Unity log line also goes to `files/BeamProRuns/app_log_<stamp>.txt`, with the times of the first glasses frame, the first valid head pose and the square placement. The logcat buffer rolls over in about 15 min, so the T6.2 log was lost |

Timecode, 3DoF and the eye chain stay as buttons for repeats.

## Session (about 10 min of runs)

1. Glasses on from the start. Say when the green text and the square appear (the app logs it as well);
   **Place square** if it is in a bad spot.
2. **"1 Guided: head loop + Eye (100 s)"**: put a mark on the floor (tape, a sheet of paper), face a wall, tap,
   follow the voice. Each step comes with a tone; three ticks mean a move is coming.
3. **"2 Guided: motion sync (40 s)"**: face a textured wall or the board, 1-2 m; follow the voice.
4. **"3 Guided: stereo pan (35 s)"**: Beam Pro in hand (glasses can stay on), follow the voice. Then **Quit EyeLab**.

The guided buttons are green on the left; the T6.2 runs stay on the right for repeats.

## Done when

- **Cues:** every step logged; Jack can run without looking at the Beam Pro.
- **B2:** loop closure and jumps per phase; still jitter per still phase; turn angles.
- **B1t:** exposure − `cam_ts` with a correlation peak clearly above its neighbours; then the age at arrival
  splits into before `cam_ts` and after.
- **BS-2:** exposure offset between sensors `0` and `2`: under about 2 ms means a usable stereo pair for
  moving scenes; one or two frames means static scenes only.
- **Glasses:** square edges steady (Jack); time to first glasses frame known.
