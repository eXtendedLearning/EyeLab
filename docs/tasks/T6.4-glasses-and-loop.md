# T6.4 — Glasses drawing again, glasses check, cleaner Beam Pro screen, any-direction loop (gates B2, glasses)

> Proposed and accepted by Jack on 2026-11-19, after the T6.3 session (`T6.3-guided-runs.md`, Results).
> Built as 0.4.004; run on 2026-11-19 (Results below). Runs ≤ 3 min (Jack, 2026-11-18).

## Why

- **The glasses showed nothing** in 0.4.003 while Unity drew. The only rendering change was MSAA 4x and
  render scale 1.0 on the Android pipeline asset (`Mobile_RPAsset`), so both are reverted to the 0.4.002
  values (MSAA off, 0.8), which drew. The soft-edged square from 0.4.003 stays: it addresses the shimmer
  without MSAA.
- **Nobody knew what the glasses showed** until Jack said so afterwards.
- **Beam Pro screen (Jack):** too cluttered, and the probe buttons overlapped the XREAL controller's App,
  trigger pad and Home buttons (their layout in the SDK prefab: App 540 px above centre, Home 770 below,
  the trigger pad 555x549 in the middle, on a 1080x2400 reference).
- **Free space (Jack):** the loop said "walk two metres forward" without warning, and depended on direction.

## Changes

| change | detail |
|---|---|
| rendering | `Mobile_RPAsset` back to MSAA off and render scale 0.8 (the 0.4.002 values) |
| XR display counters | every 5 s in the app log: display running, render passes, refresh rate, **frames presented per second**, dropped frames, app and compositor GPU time, motion-to-photon, eye texture size, render scale, Unity fps. Tells whether frames reach the glasses at all |
| glasses check | at start the voice says what should be visible; three buttons at the top of the screen, **I see text + square / Text only / Nothing**; the answer goes into the app log and every run's `run.json` (`glasses_seen`) |
| Beam Pro screen | an opaque cover over the XREAL controller (its buttons cannot be hit by mistake); one centre column: the glasses check, the three guided runs, Place square ahead, **Archive** (the older runs, with Back), Quit |
| loop cues | the first prompt says the space needed: about 1 m of free floor on **any one side**; the walk is two steps towards whichever side is free, a 4 s stop, back onto the mark facing ahead. No direction, no turning round. About 95 s |
| sync, pan cues | say "stand anywhere" / "anywhere" first; sync still needs a textured wall 1-2 m ahead |
| analyser | the loop also reports how far the walk went (the closure is read against it) |

## Session (about 3 min of runs)

1. Glasses on. Start EyeLab from MyGlasses, tap **App**. When the voice asks, tap what you see at the top of
   the screen.
2. **1 Head loop + Eye (95 s)**: put a mark on the floor anywhere with 1 m free on some side; follow the voice.
3. **2 Motion sync (40 s)**: face a textured wall or the board 1-2 m away.
4. **3 Stereo pan (35 s)**: Beam Pro in hand. Then **Quit EyeLab**.

## Done when

- **Glasses:** text and square visible (Jack's answer), presented frames ≈ the refresh rate in the log. If
  not: the counters say whether frames reach the compositor, which separates an app problem from a glasses one.
- **B2:** a second loop closure, with a shorter walk (about 1 m), to compare with 259 mm after 2 m.
- **Repeat check:** motion sync and pan once more, to see that −2.5 ms and the 80 ms sensor offset hold. The Eye
  age should be compared with the glasses drawing (110 ms in T6.3 while they were not).

## Results (2026-11-19 session, 0.4.004)

Runs `20261119_145137_loop`, `20261119_145316_sync` and `20261119_145403_pan`, plus `app_log_20261119_145112.txt`.

- **Glasses draw again.** First glasses frame 8.0 s after start, square placed at 8.3 s. Jack answered
  **I see text + square** at 21 s. Reverting MSAA 4x and render scale 1.0 fixed the empty glasses.
- **The square flickers (Jack).** The display counters show the likely mechanism. The glasses refresh at
  60 Hz, but Unity draws only **41-49 fps**, idle as well as in runs (the watchdog shows 45 fps before any
  probe starts).
  - The present count is 1 or 2 per frame: some frames are shown twice, so world-fixed content stutters
    against the head motion.
  - In 0.4.003, with nothing reaching the glasses, Unity ran at 58 fps. So drawing the stereo view is what
    costs the 13 fps.
  - Dropped frames read a constant 60-61 per sample and motion-to-photon about 33 ms. App and compositor GPU
    times are not reported by the XREAL provider.
  - Not separated yet: frame pacing (45 on 60) versus aliasing (render scale 0.8, eye texture 1968x1134, no
    MSAA). This is the next gate (T6.5).
- **Loop (B2).**
  - Walk: 851 mm from the mark. Closure: **84 mm horizontal**, yaw +0.4 deg, no jumps.
  - **Height: +104 mm.** The height did not move during the walk. It rose by about 120 mm during the
    look up / look down / look centre steps and stayed there, from -27 mm in still_1 to +80 mm from the
    turns onwards.
  - A head pitch cannot lift the glasses 10 cm, so this is a tracker error tied to looking at the floor or
    ceiling. T6.3 had +30 mm. It matters between marker sightings, not once ArUco re-registers.
- **Motion sync.** Eye exposure − `cam_ts` = **−0.5 ms** (R² 0.993, 1070 pairs); T6.3 had −2.5 ms. `cam_ts`
  is the exposure time within about 3 ms.
- **Eye frame age.** 133 ms in the loop and 138 ms in sync while the glasses drew, against 110 ms in T6.3
  while they did not. Drawing costs about 25 ms of Eye latency as well as fps.
- **Stereo pan.**
  - Sensor 2 versus sensor 0: **+14.5 ms** this time, against about 80 ms in T6.3. The two physical result
    timestamps differ by a median 0 ms, p95 70 ms.
  - The fits are wide (peak width about 90 ms, R² changes by 0.001 at ±10 ms), so neither number is sharp.
  - Either way the offset is not fixed: the rear pair stays still-scenes only.
