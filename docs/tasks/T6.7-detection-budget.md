# T6.7 — Detection within budget on the Beam Pro

> Written 2026-11-20, built as 0.4.009 (plugin ABI 3). Run 1 (2026-11-22): the region at 1 thread is the only mode
> near the render margin (5-7 % late); plate A is too small at 75 cm; plate L's non-planar solves can diverge. Next: T6.8. Parent: T6.6 run 2
> (`docs/tasks/T6.6-b3a-detection.md`): cost, parity and age were met, but the render margin was not, because the
> detection worker was busy on 99 % of Unity frames. One run of about 2.5 min plus a bench of a few seconds.
> Needs plate A (markers 30-32, 20 mm), plate L (markers 40-43, 20 mm) and the 25 mm ChArUco board (5×7, 19 mm markers, ids 0-16).
> The cardboard cube is deformed and not used (Jack, 2026-11-20).

## Why

T6.6 run 2 showed that the glasses' render loop loses its margin while detection runs on every Eye frame:
- late Unity frames rose to 11-19 %, against 2.8 % when the worker is idle;
- plate A's poses were IPPE-ambiguous in 35-67 % of frames;
- about 10 % of still poses took the wrong solution.

T6.7 asks:
1. Which way of spending less CPU keeps the margin with a pose at 15 Hz or more?
   - a fixed rate cap;
   - a region of interest (ROI) around the target;
   - a lower worker priority.
2. Does a trust rule remove the wrong-solution poses?
3. Do plate L and the board (more points, not coplanar for L) avoid the ambiguity?

## Replay of run 2 before building (offline, run 2's corners)

- **Most wrong poses are 2-marker poses.** In `t2_still`, 44 of the 58 wrong poses used two markers. For only 8 of those was
  IPPE's other solution the right one, so choosing the solution cannot fix them.
- **A prior makes it worse.** Picking the solution nearest the last trusted pose let one wrong pose seed the next: wrong poses
  went from 56 to 147.
- **PoseLock's rule works:**
  - trust a planar pose only when IPPE's ratio is ≥ 3 or the two solutions are within 10° (`PoseLockConfig`);
  - this left 0-2 wrong poses and kept 52-60 % of them.

So the device marks each pose **trusted** by that rule and does not use a prior. The plugin still accepts a prior
(`ela_process_roi`), and reports whether it was used, for later tests.

### Modes

| mode | what runs | threads | nice |
|---|---|---|---|
| full | every Eye frame, whole frame (T6.6) | 2 | 0 |
| cap | whole frame, at most 15 Hz (frames in between skipped and counted) | 2 | 0 |
| roi | the region around the last projected box (box × 0.5 margin, at least 48 px). Whole frame when there is no box, when the box is older than 0.5 s, or after the target is lost inside a region | 1 | 10 |

### ROI and CLAHE

CLAHE's 8×8 tiles cover the crop, not the frame, so the contrast and hence some sub-pixel corners change: up to
about 1 px on synthetic frames. Against the true projection the error is the same as on the full frame (1.880 px both). Consequences:
- full-frame parity is checked as in T6.6;
- region parity is checked against Python on **the same crop**;
- the device's own region-against-full difference is reported, not gated.

### Report (`python -m xreal_gates bp <run>`)

- **Bench:**
  - cost by mode and threads;
  - region share of the frame;
  - full parity, region parity on the same crop, and the device's region against the full frame.
- **Live, per step:**
  - cost, rate and age;
  - the render margin split by worker busy or idle;
  - a mode line: region share, frames skipped by the cap, trusted share.
- **Still steps:**
  - wrong-solution poses (normal more than 10° from the median), among all poses and among trusted ones;
  - trusted high-pass jitter;
  - the head's position spread at the same frames.
- **Parity:** eye frames, full and roi, per target.

## Done when

- **Margin:** late Unity frames ≤ 4 % in at least one mode, with a pose at ≥ 15 Hz on still steps.
- **Trust:** no wrong-solution pose among trusted still poses, for each target.
- **Parity unchanged:**
  - exact on the bench full frames;
  - exact against Python on the same region crop.
- **Next step named:** B3b (the pose in the glasses, corrected with the head pose at `cam_ts`), with the mode and target chosen here.

## Run 1 (2026-11-22, 0.4.009, plugin ABI 3)

Run `20261122_095312_budget`, analysed with `python -m xreal_gates bp`. Glasses seen: all. Eye frames 4355,
processed 2776, skipped while busy 1546.

### Cost

Bench (median ms per frame, 1 thread unless said):

| mode | all frames | real Eye frames with plate A |
|---|---|---|
| full, 1 thread | 47.6 | 79.0 |
| full, 2 threads | 31.6 | 43.0 |
| region, 1 thread (20 % of the frame, median) | 17.7 | 29.4 |

Live, still steps:

| step | mode | poses/s | processing ms | age ms | late Unity frames |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a_full_still | full, 2 threads | 15.4 | 65.8 | 181 | 13.2 % |
| a_cap_still | full, 2 threads, 15 Hz cap | 13.3 | 68.6 | 183 | 12.0 % |
| a_roi_still | region, 1 thread, nice 10 | 28.1 | 35.0 | 148 | 5.1 % |
| l_roi_still | region | 27.4 | 33.1 | 137 | 6.7 % |
| b_roi_still | region | 29.3 | 25.6 | 124 | 5.3 % |

- **The cap does not help.** A full frame at 2 threads takes 69 ms live, so at 15 Hz the worker is still busy on 92 % of
  Unity frames. Late frames follow the threads in use: 12-18 % with full frames at 2 threads, 4-7 % with the region at 1.
- **The region costs 25-35 ms live,** not the 16 % of the full frame its area suggests. In an offline replay on
  this run's 278 saved Eye frames, scaling ArUco's perimeter rates for the crop (they are fractions of the image side, so a
  crop lets smaller contours through) cut detection by 30 % with the same markers found. The adaptive-threshold window step
  changes the full frame's cost by under 1 %.
- **Moving steps** find the target on 30-50 % of frames. A lost region falls back to a full frame at 1 thread
  and nice 10, which costs 94-112 ms.
- **Age:** 124-148 ms with the region (arrival 95-97), against 176-183 ms with full frames.

### Pose quality

| target | still poses | IPPE-ambiguous | wrong solution, all | trusted | wrong among trusted | trusted high-pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plate A, 755 mm | 132-338 per step | 82-89 % | 25-28 % | 11-18 % | 0-1 | 3.4-4.2 mm (cap, region) |
| board, 547 mm | 349 | 89 % | 1 % (3) | 11 % | 0 | 7.0 mm (all poses 2.8) |
| plate L, 708 mm | 327 | 97 % of planar | 15 % | 15 % | 17 of 50 | — |

- **Plate A at 75 cm is too small.** Its 20 mm markers are about 18 px wide, so both IPPE solutions sit inside the corner
  noise. A replay that picks the solution nearest a room-fixed normal (the head pose at `cam_ts` with the Unity↔OpenCV flip,
  reference from trusted poses only) lowered the wrong share only from 115 to 91 on `a_roi_still`. These are not clean
  flips that a prior can fix.
- **The board is good** (1 % wrong, 2.8 mm high-pass). However, the ratio rule trusts only 11 % of its poses: near-frontal and
  far, IPPE's two errors stay close even when the best one is right.
- **Plate L standing on the table:** face H (markers 40, 41, normal up) was edge-on. 238 of the 327 still poses came from
  markers 42 and 43 alone. Its non-planar solves (face V plus one face H marker) sometimes diverged, with RMS up to 2198 px.
  The device trusted every non-planar pose, so these passed. PoseLock's `max_acquire_reproj_rms_px` (15 px) would have
  rejected them.
- **Head pose at `cam_ts`:** high-pass 0.06-0.31 mm on still steps. The head is far steadier than any pose.

### Parity

- **Bench full frames:** exact (0.0009 px, 0.001 mm).
- **Live full frames:** exact on 50/55.
- **Bench region against Python on the same crop:** exact on 20/23, up to 0.74 px (0.22 mm) on 3.
- **Live region:** exact on 110/157, and the ids are equal on 158/163.

Padding the crop with real neighbours instead of reflection does not explain the differences, and neither does the
thread count. Full frames (1280×720, whole 8×8 CLAHE tiles) match exactly, and the differing regions all had sizes that
are not multiples of 8. The likely cause is CLAHE's interpolation over fractional tiles rounding differently on ARM.
From 0.4.010 (plugin ABI 4) every region is grown to multiples of 8.
