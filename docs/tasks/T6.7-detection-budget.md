# T6.7 — Detection within budget on the Beam Pro

> Written 2026-11-20, built as 0.4.009 (plugin ABI 3). Not run yet. Parent: T6.6 run 2
> (`docs/tasks/T6.6-b3a-detection.md`): cost, parity and age were met, but the render margin was not, because the
> detection worker was busy on 99 % of Unity frames. One run of about 2.5 min plus a bench of a few seconds.
> Needs plate A (markers 30-32, 20 mm), plate L (markers 40-43, 20 mm) and the 25 mm ChArUco board (5×7, 19 mm markers, ids 0-16).
> The cardboard cube is deformed and not used (Jack, 2026-11-20).

## Why

T6.6 run 2 showed that the glasses' render loop loses its margin while detection runs on every Eye frame:
- late Unity frames rose to 11-19 %, against 2.8 % when the worker is idle;
- plate A's poses were IPPE-ambiguous in 35-67 % of frames;
- about 10 % of still poses took the wrong solution.

T6.7 asks:
1. Which way of spending less CPU keeps the margin with a pose at 15 Hz or more?
   - a fixed rate cap;
   - a region of interest (ROI) around the target;
   - a lower worker priority.
2. Does a trust rule remove the wrong-solution poses?
3. Do plate L and the board (more points, not coplanar for L) avoid the ambiguity?

## Replay of run 2 before building (offline, run 2's corners)

- **Most wrong poses are 2-marker poses.** In `t2_still`, 44 of the 58 wrong poses used two markers. For only 8 of those was
  IPPE's other solution the right one, so choosing the solution cannot fix them.
- **A prior makes it worse.** Picking the solution nearest the last trusted pose let one wrong pose seed the next: wrong poses
  went from 56 to 147.
- **PoseLock's rule works:**
  - trust a planar pose only when IPPE's ratio is ≥ 3 or the two solutions are within 10° (`PoseLockConfig`);
  - this left 0-2 wrong poses and kept 52-60 % of them.

So the device marks each pose **trusted** by that rule and does not use a prior. The plugin still accepts a prior
(`ela_process_roi`), and reports whether it was used, for later tests.

### Modes

| mode | what runs | threads | nice |
|---|---|---|---|
| full | every Eye frame, whole frame (T6.6) | 2 | 0 |
| cap | whole frame, at most 15 Hz (frames in between skipped and counted) | 2 | 0 |
| roi | the region around the last projected box (box × 0.5 margin, at least 48 px). Whole frame when there is no box, when the box is older than 0.5 s, or after the target is lost inside a region | 1 | 10 |

### ROI and CLAHE

CLAHE's 8×8 tiles cover the crop, not the frame, so the contrast and hence some sub-pixel corners change: up to
about 1 px on synthetic frames. Against the true projection the error is the same as on the full frame (1.880 px both). Consequences:
- full-frame parity is checked as in T6.6;
- region parity is checked against Python on **the same crop**;
- the device's own region-against-full difference is reported, not gated.

### Protocol (about 147 s, voice-guided)

| step | target | mode | s |
|---|---|---|---|
| a_ready | plate A straight ahead, 60 cm | full | 8 |
| a_full_still, a_cap_still, a_roi_still | hold still (three modes, same scene) | full, cap, roi | 10, 10, 12 |
| a_roi_move | slow head moves, plate in view | roi | 12 |
| a_roi_away, a_roi_back | look away, then back | roi | 5, 8 |
| a_full_move | slow head moves | full | 10 |
| l_ready, l_roi_still, l_roi_move | plate L where A was, both faces towards you | roi | 12, 12, 12 |
| b_ready, b_roi_still, b_roi_move | the board, upright, 60 cm | roi | 12, 12, 12 |

The bench runs first, on the pushed frame set: full at 1 and 2 threads, then roi at 1 thread around the full frame's box.

### Report (`python -m xreal_gates bp <run>`)

- **Bench:**
  - cost by mode and threads;
  - region share of the frame;
  - full parity, region parity on the same crop, and the device's region against the full frame.
- **Live, per step:**
  - cost, rate and age;
  - the render margin split by worker busy or idle;
  - a mode line: region share, frames skipped by the cap, trusted share.
- **Still steps:**
  - wrong-solution poses (normal more than 10° from the median), among all poses and among trusted ones;
  - trusted high-pass jitter;
  - the head's position spread at the same frames.
- **Parity:** eye frames, full and roi, per target.

## Done when

- **Margin:** late Unity frames ≤ 4 % in at least one mode, with a pose at ≥ 15 Hz on still steps.
- **Trust:** no wrong-solution pose among trusted still poses, for each target.
- **Parity unchanged:**
  - exact on the bench full frames;
  - exact against Python on the same region crop.
- **Next step named:** B3b (the pose in the glasses, corrected with the head pose at `cam_ts`), with the mode and target chosen here.
