> Written 2026-11-22, built as 0.4.012. Run 1 (2026-11-22): the drawing is room-fixed to 1-6 mm and "very stable",
> but clearly off the markers; camera and head chain verified, so the offset is the display's. Next: T6.10
> (`docs/tasks/T6.10-align-by-eye.md`).
> Parent: T6.8 run 1 (`docs/tasks/T6.8-lean-detection.md`).
> One run of about 2 min plus the bench. Needs plate L (markers 40-43, 20 mm), tilted about 45° so both faces show,
> and the 25 mm ChArUco board (5×7, 19 mm markers, ids 0-16). Plate A and the cube are not used.

## Why

T6.8 run 1 settled detection:
- the region at every Eye frame, 1 thread, nice 10, gives 27.6 poses/s;
- late frames are at the idle-worker level;
- plate L tilted gives non-planar poses with no wrong trusted pose.

The next question is the one EyeLab exists for. When the pose is drawn in the glasses, does it sit on the specimen and
stay there while the head moves? That is B3b: the pose carried into the room with the head pose at the frame's
exposure (`cam_ts`), as proven on the Surface (`docs/BEAMPRO-HANDOVER-2026-11-10.md`, turning jump 1.3-1.8 mm).

## The chain

```
world = head(cam_ts) · rgb_from_head · (F·R·F, F·t)
```

| link | source | checked |
|---|---|---|
| `R, t` | the plugin's pose of the target in the Eye camera (OpenCV, metres) | parity with Python (T6.6-T6.8) |
| `F = diag(1, −1, 1)` | OpenCV to Unity (THEORY §6.3, PC gate `CvRotationToUnity`) | Surface G5 |
| `rgb_from_head` | `XREALPlugin.GetDevicePoseFromHead(XREAL_COMPONENT_RGB_CAMERA)`, read once per run, in `run.json` | new on the Beam Pro |
| `head(cam_ts)` | the native `GetHeadPoseAtTime(cam_ts)`: Unity's tracking pose with z flipped, position (x, y, −z), rotation S·R·S, S = diag(1, 1, −1) | **fitted 2026-11-22** on the T6.3 loop run (2 m walk): 0.0 mm and 0.01° median against Unity's head pose; identity and the x and y flips are 178-262 mm off |

The drawing jumps to each trusted pose, with no filter, so the wearer sees the raw chain. The wearer's view offset (the
PC's 1.3° boresight, set by eye) is not applied yet. If the drawing is off by a fixed angle, that is the next gate.

### Found while building

- **The T6.8 report's room normal used the wrong flip** (`F` on the native head rotation). Corrected to the fitted
  convention in 0.4.012; T6.8's numbers barely change (plate L `l_r15_still`: 2 wrong of 119, was 1).
- **5 trusted plate L poses in T6.8 run 1 were behind the camera** (t_z ≈ −0.56 m, RMS 0.6-3 px): non-planar solves
  through the camera centre. In the room they sat 1.2 m off. The trust rule now needs t_z > 0. On T6.8's data without
  them, plate L stays within 8 mm in the room while the head moves 10-13 cm, and within 3.0 mm (1.0 mm high-pass) held
  still, with `rgb_from_head` taken as identity.

### The scale fit

A wrong marker size, K or unit factor scales the camera translation only, and reprojects perfectly (AGENTS.md
invariant). In the room, it shows as the target moving when the head translates. The report fits the factor `s` on the
camera translation that keeps the target stillest over a step where the head moved more than 5 cm. `s` far from 1 names
a scale error, or an `rgb_from_head` lever arm error, which are separated only by moving more in translation than in
rotation.

- **The drawing is room-fixed to a few millimetres.** Most of the still std is a slow drift of about 3 mm along the line
  of sight over 10 s, on both targets. That is 6DoF tracking drift, as on the Surface (§26), not detection noise
  (high-pass 0.6-1.0 mm). Plate L's across-view part (5 mm) follows its marker count switching between 3 and 4.
- **The device's chain matches Python exactly:** 1426 shown poses, 0.000 mm.
- **Exposure to drawing: 138 ms median** (169 p95), applied on the main thread.
- **Wrong solutions:** 0 among 297 trusted plate L still poses, 0 among 301 on the board. The board is now trusted on
  99 % of still frames (rule: 12 markers or more).
- **Scale fit:** 1.14 (plate L move), 1.50 (board move). Weakly determined: it lowers the std only from 3.6 to 2.9 mm
  and from 5.4 to 3.7 mm, because the head turned as much as it moved. Inconclusive, not evidence of a scale error.

### Why the drawing is off

The camera side checks out:
- **Intrinsics:** the SDK's Eye intrinsics on the Beam Pro are the Surface's to 0.1 px (fx 607.23, fy 607.40,
  cx 638.26, cy 359.81, 1280×720). The same sensor and crop, so the Surface calibration (fx 623.9) holds.
- **Camera-from-head:** `rgb_from_head` puts the Eye 28 mm ahead of the head and rotated 1.3°, as on the Surface.
- **Room-fixed:** the drawing does not swim, so the head pose at `cam_ts` and its convention are right.

What is left is the display side: XREAL's eye and display model against Jack's eyes. On the Surface this was a view
offset of about 1.3°, set by eye (§23-24, `ViewOffset.cs`). That is about 15 mm at 65 cm, so "clearly off". Ratings:
*clearly off* on 5 of 7, *a few mm off* on 2.
