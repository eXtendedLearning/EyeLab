# T6.5 — Render gate: why the square flickers, and which settings give a stable image

> Asked for by Jack on 2026-11-19 after the T6.4 session ("I see the box but with flickering"), as an extra
> gate next to B3. Built as 0.4.005; run on 2026-11-19 (Results below). From 0.4.006 the run has five settings
> (about 85 s): SMAA and MSAA are gone.
> ADR-0004 (accepted the same day) puts **operable** first: a very stable image and the lowest latency. A
> setting that looks better but costs frame rate is judged against that.

## Why

In T6.4 the glasses drew, but the room-fixed square flickered. The app log shows:

- Unity draws **41-49 fps** against a **60 Hz** display, idle as well as in runs. The compositor shows some
  frames twice (present count 1 or 2).
- Rendering is at **0.8 render scale**, with no MSAA, HDR on, post-processing on and shadows on (URP template
  defaults).

Two causes are possible, and the counters alone cannot separate them:

| cause | what it predicts |
|---|---|
| **frame pacing** (fps below the refresh rate) | room-fixed content stutters or doubles while the head moves; head-locked content is steady; a setting that reaches 60 fps is steady |
| **aliasing** (render scale 0.8, no anti-aliasing) | edges crawl even head-locked and when still; MSAA or SMAA helps the hard grid; the soft, mipmapped square suffers less |

The square is a soft-edged mipmapped texture: MSAA and SMAA act on geometry edges, not on a texture's alpha.
The measurement overlay will be thin geometry. So the glasses now also show a **hard grid** (40 cm, 3 mm
strips and two diagonals) to the right of the square.

## The run (button **4 Rendering**)

Settings are switched live and announced by number only, so the rating is blind. Each setting lasts 15 s:
hold still 5 s, turn slowly 6 s, rate 4 s. During the run the Beam Pro screen shows four buttons:
**Steady / Edges shimmer / Flicker or judder / Nothing visible**. The last tap within a setting is its rating.

| # | id | render scale | MSAA | HDR, post, shadows | other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `now` | 0.8 | off | on | the 0.4.004 settings |
| 2 | `head_locked` | 0.8 | off | on | square and grid held 1.5 m ahead of the head |
| 3 | `lean` | 0.8 | off | off | |
| 4 | `lean_06` | 0.6 | off | off | does fps reach 60? |
| 5 | `lean_10` | 1.0 | off | off | |
| 6 | `smaa` | 0.8 | off | off | post-processing with SMAA only (volume off) |
| 7 | `msaa_2` | 0.8 | 2x | off | last: 4x emptied the glasses in 0.4.003 |
| 8 | `lean_again` | 0.8 | off | off | do the glasses come back after MSAA? |

At the end the settings are restored to what they were before the run.

Every frame goes to `render.csv`:

- the setting and cue step, Unity's frame time;
- the XR display's present count, dropped count and motion-to-photon;
- XREAL's extended, early and teared frame counters (`XRStats`);
- render scale and MSAA.

The run's `run.json` also records XREAL's own target frame rate (`XREALPlugin.GetTargetFrameRate`).

Analyse with `python -m xreal_gates bp <run>`. It prints one line per setting: fps, frame time median and
p95, the share of frames over one refresh, frames shown once and twice or more, motion-to-photon, the counter
deltas, and the rating.

## Session

After **Quit**, start EyeLab again from MyGlasses. Answer the glasses check, then tap **4 Rendering** and follow
the voice. Stand anywhere; no walking is needed. If the glasses go blank at setting 7, wait for setting 8 and
rate it.

## Done when

- **Cause named:** pacing, aliasing or both, from the ratings against fps and the present counts.
- **A default chosen** for the overlay build: the cheapest setting rated *steady* for both the square and the
  grid, with its fps. If none is steady, the next step follows from the cause:
  - pacing: cut GPU work further, or try XREAL's target frame rate;
  - aliasing: geometry and line-width rules for the overlay.
- **Margin recorded** for ADR-0004's portability: the fps of the chosen setting, against 60, on the Beam Pro.
  B3a's detection must fit beside it.

## Results (2026-11-19 session, 0.4.005, run `20261119_174142_render`)

Glasses check "all" at 19 s. XREAL's own target frame rate reads −1 (not set); Unity's is 120.

| # | setting | fps | frame time med/p95 ms | > 1 refresh | shown 2x+ | m2p ms | Jack's rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `now` (0.4.004) | 45.2 | 17.9 / 35.7 | 35 % | 37 % | 33.0 | not rated (he said: flickers) |
| 2 | `head_locked` | 44.7 | 17.8 / 35.9 | 36 % | 25 % | 32.9 | steady |
| 3 | `lean` | **59.9** | 16.9 / 20.3 | 2.8 % | 24 % | 32.9 | steady |
| 4 | `lean_06` | 59.9 | 16.8 / 21.0 | 5.6 % | 14 % | 32.6 | steady |
| 5 | `lean_10` | 59.9 | 16.8 / 20.8 | 4.9 % | 0 % | 31.2 | steady |
| 6 | `smaa` | 31.7 | 33.2 / 40.7 | 86 % | 36 % | 31.7 | edges shimmer / flicker or judder |
| 7 | `msaa_2` | 59.8 | 16.8 / 20.6 | 4.7 % | 25 % | 32.2 | **nothing visible** |
| 8 | `lean_again` | 59.9 | 16.8 / 20.4 | 4.1 % | 7 % | 32.3 | **nothing visible** |

- **Cause: frame pacing.** The 0.4.004 settings draw 45 fps, and a third of the frames take two refreshes. The
  same content head-locked was rated steady at the same 45 fps, so the flicker is room-fixed content juddering
  while the head moves, not edge aliasing. HDR, post-processing and shadows (URP template defaults that EyeLab
  never uses) cost the missing 15 fps; without them Unity holds 59.9 fps at render scale 0.6, 0.8 and 1.0, and
  all three were rated steady. Jack: "We have stability in 2 to 5."
- **SMAA** halves the frame rate (post-processing pass) and brings the judder back. Not used.
- **MSAA blanks the glasses, and they stay blank.** Twice now (4x in 0.4.003, 2x here): Unity keeps drawing at
  60 fps and the XR display reports frames presented, but nothing reaches the glasses. Setting 8 (MSAA off
  again) stayed blank, and so did a restart of EyeLab (app log `174414`: XR display running, 49 fps, glasses
  check "nothing"). Recovery needs the glasses reconnected (or the Beam Pro restarted); that is Jack's. MSAA
  is banned: `RenderProbe.Apply` throws on it. (Jack remembered setting 6 as the black one; his taps put it
  at 7.)
- `present` count and the teared counter do not track the ratings well (`lean` 24 % shown twice at 59.9 fps,
  `lean_10` 0 %); motion-to-photon is 31-33 ms in every setting. Treat them as context, not as the measure.

**Default from 0.4.006: `lean` at render scale 0.8**, applied at start-up by `RenderProbe.ApplyDefaults` (URP HDR,
MSAA, camera post-processing, shadows, anti-aliasing and the global volume off). 0.8 rather than 1.0: both are
rated steady at 59.9 fps, and 0.8 keeps more GPU headroom (fewer frames over one refresh: 2.8 % against 4.9 %);
the overlay's thin lines may ask for 1.0, which T6.6 and the overlay build revisit.

**Margin for ADR-0004:** fps is capped by the 60 Hz display, so it cannot show headroom by itself; the frame
time p95 (20 ms at 0.8) and the share over one refresh are the measure. B3a (T6.6) runs detection beside this
setting and reports the same two numbers.
