# T6.13 — The Eye camera calibrated on the Beam Pro, with a tape check

> Written 2026-11-23, built as 0.4.016. Parent: T6.12 run 1 (`docs/tasks/T6.12-eye-calibration.md`): a tape check found
> the device's Eye camera model 25-28 % short in focal length.
>
> One run of about 4 minutes. The glasses lie on the table (not worn). You need the 25 mm ChArUco board and a tape
> measure. The last step of 0.4: T6.12 is run again afterwards under the new camera model.

## Why

Every Beam Pro probe posed with the set's camera model: the Surface PC-gate calibration of 2026-10-21 (fx 624 px at
1280×720), which the vendor's own intrinsics (fx 607) roughly match. On the Beam Pro, a tape check (T6.12 run 1) put the
board's plane at:
- 398.7 mm with the lens at 500 mm;
- 785.7 mm with the lens at 1000 mm.

That needs fx about 790 px. A focal-length error scales every distance from the camera and reprojects perfectly, so
nothing on the device showed it (AGENTS.md: check an absolute distance). It explains:
- T6.9's constant offset;
- T6.10's opposite-eye error;
- T6.11's 42 mm "eye spacing";
- T6.12's "swims when I move".

Along the way:
- **The board print** measures 25.97 mm per square (caliper, 2026-11-23), 3.9 % over nominal. Poses of board25 now use
  the printed size: `gui_common.CHARUCO_SQUARE_PRINTED_M`, one definition. The nominal size still draws the board.
- **`rgb_from_head`** is now logged by every variant (T6.11's `spaam` variant logged none).

### The tape check

The scale is taken from the *difference* of the two tape distances:
`scale = (1000 − 500) / (d100 − d50)`, where d50 and d100 are the board-plane distances under the new model.

That does not depend on where the camera's centre sits behind the lens. The offset `tape − d` estimates that distance,
which should be a few millimetres and the same at both distances.

A calibration is accepted when:
- |scale − 1| ≤ 3 %;
- the views cover at least 35 % of the image.

## Protocol (about 4 min, voice-guided)

| step | s | what Jack does |
|---|---|---|
| s_ready | 14 | glasses on the table, lenses facing you, still; board in both hands about 40 cm in front |
| m_centre | 8 | board in the middle of the camera's view, facing it |
| m_tl, m_tr, m_bl, m_br | 10 each | into each corner of the view, tilted a little ("your right" is the camera's left) |
| m_edges | 10 | slowly along all four edges |
| m_tilt_v, m_tilt_h | 10 each | tilt the top towards and away, then turn left and right, about 45° |
| m_near, m_far | 10 each | about 25 cm, then about 70 cm, moving and tilted |
| t50_place | ≤ 90 | board upright on the table; set the lens **exactly 50.0 cm** from the board with the tape, facing it; **NEXT** |
| t50 | 10 | keep still |
| t100_place, t100 | ≤ 90, 10 | the same at **exactly 100.0 cm** |

After the run, the agent runs `bp-calib`, writes the profile, updates the frame set and pushes it to `files/b3a/`. Then
come T6.12 Eye calibration and Check only.

## Done when

- **Calibration:**
  - RMS below 0.6 px;
  - coverage at least 35 %;
  - fx near the tape check's 790 px (expected 770-810).
- **Tape check under the new model:** scale within 3 % of 1, the lens-to-centre offset a few mm and equal at both
  distances.
- **Pushed:** the frame set carries the new model, and the next run's `set` line names it.
- **T6.12 again (Eye calibration, then Check only after re-wearing):**
  - "This round" is *on the markers* at 35-60 cm;
  - the SDK is closer than before (most of its error was the camera);
  - "swims when I move" is gone or rare;
  - after re-wearing, "All rounds" is still *on the markers* or *a few mm off*.
- **0.4 closes:** a MINOR cut to 0.5.000 with the result (`docs/VERSIONING.md`).
