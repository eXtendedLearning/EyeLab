# T6.2 — Head pose on the Beam Pro, timecode redo, stereo open (gates B2, B1t, BS-2)

> Proposed 2026-11-18 after the first B1 runs (`T6.1-eye-chain-latency.md`, Results); accepted by Jack the same
> day. Built as 0.4.002 and installed on the Beam Pro; not run yet.
> Parent: `docs/BEAMPRO-HANDOVER-2026-11-10.md` gate table (B2). **Run length rule (Jack, 2026-11-18):** every
> run ≤ 3 min, normally 1-2; several short runs rather than one long one.

## Why these three

- **B2** is the next gate of the hand-over: the overlay needs the head pose, and on the Surface the Eye stream
  and 6DoF together were the failure point (rows 17-18 of `CAPABILITIES.md`: throttling, a growing queue).
  B1 shows the Beam Pro has no queue; B2 checks that this holds with tracking on, and that each Eye frame can
  be paired with the head pose at its own `cam_ts` (`GetHeadPoseAtTime`, exported by the Android
  `libXREALXRPlugin.so` as on Windows).
- **B1t** finishes B1's question 4: what `cam_ts` stamps and how long exposure → arrival really is. The age
  of 152 ms is arrival − `cam_ts`; the timecode tells how far `cam_ts` is from the light.
- **BS-2** costs one 30 s run: B1's inventory showed the hidden logical rear pair (id `3` = `0` + `2`) answers
  by name. Whether an app may open it decides whether the Beam Pro can be a stereo observer at all.

## Probe changes (one APK, buttons on the Beam Pro screen as now)

| change | detail |
|---|---|
| chain default 2 min | was 5 min; age was flat within the first minute, 2 min is enough for drift |
| **Head pose** probe (new, `HeadPoseProbe.cs`) | every Update: head pose and tracking state from the XR input device, `GetTrackingType`, the HMD clock (`head.csv`); events: mode switch requested and done with its duration, tracked/untracked, jumps > 10 cm in one frame (`events.csv`). With the Eye on: for each kept frame, `GetHeadPoseAtTime(cam_ts)` next to the pose at arrival (`eye_pose.csv`), so the head motion inside the frame age is measured. Each head run sets its own mode first; the 3DoF run switches back to 6DoF at the end |
| **Timecode** fixed | each id held for ≥ 100 ms (3 camera frames); marker grey (about 60 %) on black, not white on white; analysis uses only the first frame that shows a new id. Draw times still logged per Unity frame |
| **Stereo open** probe (new, Java) | opens logical camera `3` with one small YUV stream per physical id (`0`, `2`), 30 s, logs both sensor timestamps per pair and any refusal. Saves a few frame pairs. No other camera use at the same time |
| **glasses view** (new, `GlassesView.cs`) | Jack on 0.4.001: no status in the glasses, only Unity's sky, out of focus and moving with the head. The Probes scene has no tracked camera rig and clears to the skybox. Now the XR camera clears to black (see-through), follows the XR head pose before each render, shows the status as two short lines 2 m ahead, and places a 40 cm blue square and cross 1.5 m ahead **fixed in the room** once tracking is up: the visual check of 6DoF |

## Session (Jack, about 15 min of runs; agent pulls over wireless adb in between)

Start EyeLab from MyGlasses, tap **App**. Each run is one button; the buttons come back when a run ends.
First look in the glasses: the green status and, after a second, the blue square ahead. Say if either is
missing; the runs are still valid without them.

1. **"Timecode 60 s"** (B1t): glasses off, Eye 30 cm from the Beam Pro screen, marker filling a good part of
   the view. Room lights **on**, Beam Pro brightness about half.
2. **"Head 6DoF 2 min"** (B2a, Eye off): stand still 20 s, look around slowly 30 s, turn 90° left and back,
   two steps forward and back, still 20 s. Watch whether the blue square stays where it is.
3. **"Head 6DoF + Eye 2 min"** (B2b): the same moves.
4. **"Head 3DoF + Eye 1 min"** (B2c): the app switches to 3DoF itself (a short black flash), then back to 6DoF
   at the end. Still 20 s, look around 40 s.
5. **"Stereo 30 s (on table)"** (BS-2): Beam Pro on the table, rear cameras towards something with texture
   (the ChArUco board if at hand). Then **Quit EyeLab**.

"Eye chain 2 min" and "Inventory + IMU 20 s" stay on the screen for repeats; not needed this session.

## Done when

- **B1t:** ≥ 100 decoded first-of-id frames; draw → `cam_ts` and draw → arrival medians and p90.
  Tells whether `cam_ts` is exposure time and how much of the 152 ms is before arrival in the app.
- **B2:** tracking-ready time after start and after a mode switch; lost/relocalised events; head pose
  rate; with the Eye on: Eye fps (still ≈ 29), age (still flat), `GetHeadPoseAtTime(cam_ts)` success rate,
  and the head rotation and translation within the frame age while turning (the error a missing
  compensation would leave). Rows in `CAPABILITIES.md` like 17-18 for the Beam Pro.
- **BS-2:** opened or refused (with the error). If opened: rate of each stream and the pair timestamp
  offset (median, spread). A spread of more than a few ms means no stereo for moving scenes.
