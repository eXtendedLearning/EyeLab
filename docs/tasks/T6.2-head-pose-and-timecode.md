# T6.2 — Head pose on the Beam Pro, timecode redo, stereo open (gates B2, B1t, BS-2)

> Proposed 2026-11-18 after the first B1 runs (`T6.1-eye-chain-latency.md`, Results); accepted by Jack the same
> day. Built as 0.4.002, run by Jack the same afternoon: results below. Follow-up proposed in
> `T6.3-guided-runs.md`.
> Parent: `docs/BEAMPRO-HANDOVER-2026-11-10.md` gate table (B2). **Run length rule (Jack, 2026-11-18):** every
> run ≤ 3 min, normally 1-2; several short runs rather than one long one.

## Why these three

- **B2** is the next gate of the hand-over: the overlay needs the head pose, and on the Surface the Eye stream
  and 6DoF together were the failure point (rows 17-18 of `CAPABILITIES.md`: throttling, a growing queue).
  B1 shows the Beam Pro has no queue; B2 checks that this holds with tracking on, and that each Eye frame can
  be paired with the head pose at its own `cam_ts` (`GetHeadPoseAtTime`, exported by the Android
  `libXREALXRPlugin.so` as on Windows).
- **B1t** finishes B1's question 4: what `cam_ts` stamps and how long exposure → arrival really is. The age
  of 152 ms is arrival − `cam_ts`; the timecode tells how far `cam_ts` is from the light.
- **BS-2** costs one 30 s run: B1's inventory showed the hidden logical rear pair (id `3` = `0` + `2`) answers
  by name. Whether an app may open it decides whether the Beam Pro can be a stereo observer at all.

## Session (Jack, about 15 min of runs; agent pulls over wireless adb in between)

Start EyeLab from MyGlasses, tap **App**. Each run is one button; the buttons come back when a run ends.
First look in the glasses: the green status and, after a second, the blue square ahead. Say if either is
missing; the runs are still valid without them.

1. **"Timecode 60 s"** (B1t): glasses off, Eye 30 cm from the Beam Pro screen, marker filling a good part of
   the view. Room lights **on**, Beam Pro brightness about half.
2. **"Head 6DoF 2 min"** (B2a, Eye off): stand still 20 s, look around slowly 30 s, turn 90° left and back,
   two steps forward and back, still 20 s. Watch whether the blue square stays where it is.
3. **"Head 6DoF + Eye 2 min"** (B2b): the same moves.
4. **"Head 3DoF + Eye 1 min"** (B2c): the app switches to 3DoF itself (a short black flash), then back to 6DoF
   at the end. Still 20 s, look around 40 s.
5. **"Stereo 30 s (on table)"** (BS-2): Beam Pro on the table, rear cameras towards something with texture
   (the ChArUco board if at hand). Then **Quit EyeLab**.

"Eye chain 2 min" and "Inventory + IMU 20 s" stay on the screen for repeats; not needed this session.

## Done when

- **B1t:** ≥ 100 decoded first-of-id frames; draw → `cam_ts` and draw → arrival medians and p90.
  Tells whether `cam_ts` is exposure time and how much of the 152 ms is before arrival in the app.
- **B2:** tracking-ready time after start and after a mode switch; lost/relocalised events; head pose
  rate; with the Eye on: Eye fps (still ≈ 29), age (still flat), `GetHeadPoseAtTime(cam_ts)` success rate,
  and the head rotation and translation within the frame age while turning (the error a missing
  compensation would leave). Rows in `CAPABILITIES.md` like 17-18 for the Beam Pro.
- **BS-2:** opened or refused (with the error). If opened: rate of each stream and the pair timestamp
  offset (median, spread). A spread of more than a few ms means no stereo for moving scenes.

## Results (Jack's session 2026-11-18, 0.4.002)

Runs `20261118_144846_timecode`, `_145023_head`, `_145231_head_eye`, `_145457_head_eye_3dof`, `_145609_stereo`
(pulled to `python/beampro_runs/`, `python -m xreal_gates bp`). The Unity log was lost: the logcat main buffer
had rolled over by the time of the pull (it starts at 15:03, the app at 14:48).

**B1t, timecode** (done-when met: 523 first-of-id frames). 1160/1720 frames decoded. First frame of each id:
draw → `cam_ts` median 111 ms (p10 95, p90 131), draw → arrival 247 ms (p10 216, p90 267). The p10-p90 spread
(36 ms) is one camera frame, as expected from the 100 ms hold. So `cam_ts` is never before the light; the 95 ms
floor is Beam Pro display latency (Unity set → pixels lit, through the XREAL virtual-controller canvas) plus
whatever `cam_ts` sits after the exposure. **The timecode cannot split those two**: it needs a display whose
latency is known. Next: measure exposure against `cam_ts` with motion instead (head pose vs image motion,
T6.3), which needs no display.

**B2, head pose.**

| run | result |
|---|---|
| 6DoF, Eye off | 48.7 Hz (one per Update), tracked 100 % from the first row, no jumps > 10 cm. Jack was still looking for the square (late, see below), so the moves did not follow the protocol: no still window to judge |
| 6DoF + Eye | Eye 29.2 fps, 0 drained, age 129 ms (IQR 14), drift +0.8 ms/min: **no queue with 6DoF on** (contrast the Surface, row 18). `GetHeadPoseAtTime` answered for 3476/3476 frames at `cam_ts` and at arrival. Head motion within the frame age: rotation median 1.5°, p90 4.9°, max 23°; translation median 7 mm, p90 32 mm (1.5° is 39 mm at 1.5 m: compensation is required, and available). Still phase 0.6° and 2.3 mm over 5 s. **Three silent relocalisation jumps** of 262, 221 and 827 mm at 63-69 s while walking back after a 180° turn, tracking state "tracked" throughout; the end position differs from the start by about 0.9 m and 9 cm in height, unknown whether Jack returned to the same spot |
| 3DoF + Eye | switch to 3DoF 0.68 s (position jumps 0.89 m, to the 3DoF origin), back to 6DoF 2.0 s. Eye 29.3 fps, age 140 ms, no queue. Still 0.45° / 0.4 mm |

**BS-2, stereo: opens.** Logical camera `3` streams both physical ids (`0`, `2`) at 640x360 in one session,
809 captures in 30 s (27.3 Hz), no refusal. The frames show a real baseline (the board shifts sideways between
the two). Both images of a pair carry the **same** image timestamp; the per-physical result timestamps start
equal and settle at exactly 66.322 ms apart (two frames, constant to the nanosecond). Both are HAL stamps, not
measurements: the timestamps cannot say whether the exposures are simultaneous. Next: a pan test (T6.3).

**Glasses view (Jack):** the green status appeared only about a minute after the start, during the timecode
run (glasses off the head then); the blue square appeared after it, not aligned with the wall (by design: it
faces the start direction), and its edges and cross shimmered, while text was clean. Cause of the shimmer: the
Android pipeline asset (`Mobile_RPAsset`) renders at **render scale 0.8 with MSAA off**, so hard-edged thin
quads alias as the head moves; text is soft-edged through the font atlas. The same will hit the overlay
wireframe. Cause of the delay: not known (log lost; one candidate is the glasses' wear sensor blanking the
display while they are off the head). Jack's request: **audio cues and guided prompts**, so he watches the
glasses and not the clock.
