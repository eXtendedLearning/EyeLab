# T6.8 — Lean region detection on the Beam Pro

> Written 2026-11-22, built as 0.4.010 (plugin ABI 4). Not run yet. Parent: T6.7 run 1
> (`docs/tasks/T6.7-detection-budget.md`). One run of about 2 min plus a bench of a few seconds. Needs the 25 mm ChArUco
> board (5×7, 19 mm markers, ids 0-16) and plate L (markers 40-43, 20 mm). Plate A and the cube are not used.

## Why

T6.7 run 1 found that only the region at 1 thread comes near the render margin: 5-7 % late Unity frames at 27-29 poses/s,
against 12-18 % with full frames. That is still above the ≤ 4 % target. Three things in that run were also fixable:

- **The region cost more than its area suggests** (25-35 ms for 16 % of the frame). ArUco's perimeter limits shrink with the
  crop. Scaled back, detection was 30 % cheaper offline with the same markers found.
- **The region did not match Python exactly** on 3 of 23 bench frames, all with sizes that were not multiples of 8.
- **Plate L's non-planar solves could diverge** (RMS up to 2198 px) and were trusted. Standing on the table, its upward face
  was edge-on, so most poses came from one face only.

Plate A at 75 cm is too small for a stable normal and is dropped. The board gave 1 % wrong poses and 2.8 mm high-pass.

## Protocol (120 s, voice-guided)

| step | target | mode | s |
|---|---|---|---|
| b_ready | board upright, about 60 cm | roi | 10 |
| b_roi_still, b_r15_still | hold still | roi, roi15 | 12, 12 |
| b_roi_move, b_r15_move | slow head moves, board in view | roi, roi15 | 12, 12 |
| l_ready | plate L where the board was, **tilted about 45° towards you so both faces show** | roi | 14 |
| l_roi_still, l_r15_still | hold still | roi, roi15 | 12, 12 |
| l_roi_move, l_r15_move | slow head moves | roi, roi15 | 12, 12 |

The bench runs first on the pushed frame set, as in T6.7: full at 1 and 2 threads, then region at 1 thread.

## Done when

- **Margin:** late Unity frames ≤ 4 % on still steps in at least one region mode, with poses at ≥ 15 Hz.
- **Cost:** region processing on the device lower than T6.7 run 1's 25-35 ms, with the same share of frames finding the target.
- **Parity:**
  - bench full frames exact;
  - bench region exact against Python on the same crop (within 0.01 px on every frame).
- **Trust:** no wrong solution among trusted still poses for the board and for plate L.
- **Plate L:** non-planar poses (both faces) on most still frames.
- **Next step named:**
  - B3b, the pose in the glasses with the head pose at `cam_ts`, using the mode and target chosen here;
  - or the remaining margin, if it is still not met.
